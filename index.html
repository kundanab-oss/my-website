The 25/5 Pomodoro Method

Study uninterrupted for 25 minutes, then take a strict 5-minute break. After 4 sessions, take a longer 20-minute break to refresh your brain.

🧠

Feynman Technique

Explain complex topics in simple language as if teaching a 10-year-old. If you get stuck, review your notes until you can explain it effortlessly.

📅

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14) to lock facts permanently into long-term memory instead of cramming.

📝

Active Recall

After reading a page or topic, close the book and write down everything you remember from memory before checking for accuracy.
